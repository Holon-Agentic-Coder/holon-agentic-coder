### Step 3: record the diff and pipeline gates per iteration

- After `inspect_ci` and the unchanged conjunction verdict, `run_review_loop` calls
  `record_iteration_gates(context, IterationGates(...), ensemble_verdict)` when reviewers were dispatched (no dry run,
  no infra error, a resolved head). The head is `resolved_target.head_sha`, the head the reviewers saw after
  `_advance_past_ledger_commit`, never a later ledger-commit head.
- The diff verdict is `decide_returned_consensus(reviewers, ensemble_size, mergeable, True, blockers)` inside
  `approval_gates` (`True` on purpose, so blockers, objections and a dirty merge still refuse it but CI does not); the
  loop's own `decide_returned_consensus` call is unchanged. The fingerprint uses the loop's `approval_fingerprint_of`
  (existing patches still apply) and is computed only when the diff is approved. The pipeline gate carries
  `pipeline_state_of(ci_green, ci_status)` and `source="stage4"`.
- The Stage 4 payload gains `consensus.gates` (`gates_summary(context)`); `approved`, ledger rows and the PR comment are
  unchanged. `loop.py`: 920 to 928 lines (budget 952); no new `# noqa`.
