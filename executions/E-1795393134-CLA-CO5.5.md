  `run_calibrate`, `reconcile_records` and `promote_pending_candidates` run, and only Prettier, stage-conflict
  resolution and final-head CI are stubbed. Each acceptance criterion has a named test:
  - parity: `test_cli_produces_the_same_commits_and_ledger_rows_as_the_flow_stage` (same /calibrated subjects, same
    reconciliation rows with `action_id` and `created_at` dropped, same promoted artefacts)
  - `--report-only`: `test_report_only_skips_reconciliation_and_promotion_and_says_so`,
    `test_report_only_json_names_the_mode`
  - re-run: `test_second_run_appends_no_duplicate_rows_and_promotes_nothing`
  - `--no-commit`: `test_no_commit_leaves_head_refs_and_ledger_unchanged`
  - `--json`: `test_json_output_is_one_document_with_the_stage5_outcome`
  - also: text output, the no-verdict skip, and a parametrised `_already_reconciled` guard test
- Deviation from the plan wording: on re-run, `run_calibrate` itself still commits the report again (the same as a flow
  re-run today, and outside Stage 5's reconcile/promote steps). The re-run test therefore asserts that the report commit
  is the only new commit, alongside no new reconciliation row and `newly_promoted_count == 0`. The intent's criterion
  ("no duplicate reconciliation rows and promotes nothing new") is met.
