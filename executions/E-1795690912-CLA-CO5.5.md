Implements plan `P-1795690552-CLA-CO5.5` for intent `I-1795690529-main-push-ci-skip-tested-tree`: a push to `main` in
`test-unit.yml` or `test-stress.yml` skips the expensive steps of a job when the pushed commit is the merge commit of

`api-error`. Both workflows gain a daily `schedule` full run and `pull-requests: read`.
