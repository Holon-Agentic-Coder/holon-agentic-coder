and no other role reads or writes `/tmp/intent.json` in the container. The only adjustment is to the scope. The intent's
`scope` misses `test_host_worktree_sandbox.py`, which pins the old pair through `_remove_argv_pair` and would fail
acceptance criterion 3. Rather than reject or defer the intent, the plan adds that file to the touch set as a one-line
companion change.

  conservatively as 23.3 to allow for reviewer churn over the extra file.
