## Plan Description & Strategy

Implement a bounded end-of-flow sync loop in a new module
`apps/sandbox-executor/src/sandbox_executor/flow/end_of_flow_sync.py` that runs at the end of a flow once review
approval and calibration complete. The loop probes PR mergeability via `check_pr_mergeability`, re-probes `UNKNOWN`
states with bounded backoff (failing closed if unresolved), and while the PR is `CONFLICTING` or `DIRTY` invokes
`sync_with_target` at `SyncBoundary.FLOW_END` (recording the re-entry round in the `target_sync` ledger row) and
re-enters Stage 4 and Stage 5 on the merged head through the pipeline engine's existing stage dispatch and
`calibrate_with_review_restart`. After at most three re-entry rounds, or on any blocked sync or non-approved re-review,
the flow blocks fail-closed with a resumable checkpoint. `pipeline.py` calls `finish_flow` before printing the final
completion message so `"completed successfully"` is emitted only when the PR is verified clean and mergeable, and
`cli/commands/flow.py` suppresses the `[REVIEW APPROVED]` banner when end-of-flow sync blocks. All new public symbols
are registered in `flow/lazy_exports_sync.py` and `flow/__init__.py` to satisfy the package export contract.
