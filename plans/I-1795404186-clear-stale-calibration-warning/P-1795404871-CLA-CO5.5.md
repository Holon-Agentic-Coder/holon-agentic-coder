     body. Skip the edit when the body is already identical; this is the idempotent rerun case.
   - It never raises.
6. `resolve_stale(context, pr_number, fresh_evaluated, seams)` edits every open stale-family comment (one with an id and
   no superseded marker) to `superseded_body(fresh_evaluated)`. It never posts and never raises. A repeated call edits
   nothing, because superseded comments carry the superseded marker.
7. `post_refreshed` posts once per head by checking for the refreshed marker. It never raises.
8. Keep every function within `C901 <= 10` and `PLR0913 <= 5`, with no `noqa`. The module has at most 200 lines and a
