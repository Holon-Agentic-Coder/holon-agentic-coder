  commit made before the pass. On the host, `_push_resolver_head` treats `head_sha == target.head_sha` as "no move";

  when it equalled the old head) is therefore required.

  which ends the loop, so a ledger-only head costs at most one wait, at the final iteration, which the resolver's push
  would have cost anyway.
