### Strategy Rationale

The intent is accepted as-is because it directly eliminates two compounding failure modes in Stage 4 resumes: (1)
crashing with `Resolver commit failed: nothing to commit, working tree clean` on pre-iteration carried-finding passes
that produce no code diff, and (2) committing and pushing empty `chore(review): record review ledger rows` commits on
pre-iteration failures when zero reviews ran and zero code changed (which triggers dozens of wasted CI runs across
resume attempts).

A key architectural constraint discovered during inspection is that
`apps/sandbox-executor/src/sandbox_executor/flow/review/loop.py` is currently 941 lines against a frozen budget of 950
lines in `holon-config/hygiene/module_budgets.json`, whereas `carried_findings.py` (121 lines) and `ledger_commit.py`
(139 lines) each have over 200 lines of headroom under the 350-line default budget. Additionally,
`flow/stages/review.py` invokes `ledger_commit._close_review_stage` in a `finally` block after `run_review_loop`
returns, so suppressing empty pre-iteration ledger commits must coordinate between `loop.py` and `ledger_commit.py` to
prevent the exit backstop from committing and pushing anyway. Placing the clean-tree detection, stale `FIND-CI-*` CI
re-probe/target-sync clearing, and `blocked_on` state management inside `carried_findings.py`, and placing
commit-suppression state helpers inside `ledger_commit.py`, keeps `loop.py` changes under the 950-line ceiling and
avoids touching any out-of-scope module.
