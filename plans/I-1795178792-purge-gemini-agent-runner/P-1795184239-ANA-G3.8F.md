### Strategy Rationale

The gemini-agent runner has been superseded by antigravity-agent and is unusable for flows. This plan completely removes
the gemini-agent runner, its container build targets, registry mappings, defaults, and documentation without leaving
behind any deprecation shims or special-case checks (`if agent_id == "gemini"`). When `--agent gemini-agent` or
`--agent gemini` is passed, the CLI and runner registry will cleanly reject it through the existing generic
unsupported-runner error path.

At the same time, the plan preserves everything that is not the gemini-agent runner:

1. Gemini models (`gemini-3.8-flash`, `gemini-3.8-flash-medium`, `gemini-3.8-flash-high`) remain fully supported and
   functional under `antigravity-agent`.
2. Antigravity's `~/.gemini/antigravity-cli` session directory mounts and effort coordination remain intact.
3. The `local-model` placeholder entry in `RUNNER_CAPABILITIES` remains untouched.
4. Gemini URL detection in `token_reduction/mitm_addon.py` and secret redaction patterns in `knowledge/records.py`
   remain untouched.
5. Historical ledger rows in `holon-knowledge/ledger/` and module budgets in `holon-config/hygiene/module_budgets.json`
   remain untouched.
