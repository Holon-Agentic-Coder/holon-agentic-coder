   `test_agent_runner.py`, `test_cli.py`, `test_pi_mounts.py`, `test_models_cli.py`, `test_naming.py`, and
   `test_planner_integration.py`.

   `gemini-agent` fails with explicit guidance directing users to `antigravity-agent`, while confirming
