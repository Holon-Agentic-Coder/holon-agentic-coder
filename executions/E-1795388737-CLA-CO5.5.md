### Step 1: Confirm how a backgrounded command appears after the runner exits

- Scratch check in `/tmp` (no repository change): `subprocess.Popen(["sh", "-c", "sleep 20 >/dev/null 2>&1 & exit 0"],
  start_new_session=True)`, waited, then scanned `/proc/*/stat` for `pgid == runner pid`.
- Result: runner pid 203 was reaped; pid 204 was found with `ppid 1` (re-parented to init) and `pgid 203`, and
  `/proc/204/cmdline` read `sleep 20`. The group was then killed with `os.killpg`.
- Conclusion: process-group membership alone is enough to find a `cmd &` leftover after the runner exits; the existing
  `pgid == root` member walk finds it. A command that calls `setsid` itself would leave the group and is not reported
  (documented limit).
