### Step 3: Write `scripts/check_module_budgets.py` (done)

- Generic over the configured packages; the only constants are `REPO_ROOT`, the default config path and the
  `HOLON_ALLOW_MODULE_BUDGET_CHANGE` flag name. Options: `--config`, `--base` (a file, or `none`) and `--root` (the
  repository root the package paths are under, used by the hermetic tests).
- Over-limit modules fail with "split the module into smaller modules - the budget is frozen and may not be changed".
  Overrides outside every package, naming a missing module, at or below the default, or unsorted are `::warning::`.
- The freeze compares raw bytes with the `git merge-base HEAD origin/main` copy: no merge base skips quietly, no copy
  at the base skips with a `::notice::` (this bootstrap). A difference names each change (added, removed, raised,
  lowered override, changed default or packages, or reformatted) and fails unless the flag equals `1`, which turns it
  into a notice.
- On this branch: `Module budgets passed: 71 modules in 1 packages (default 200, 22 overrides)` after the bootstrap
  notice.
