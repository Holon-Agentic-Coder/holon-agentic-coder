### Step 4: Gate wired into the review loop; applied fixes stamped with their commit

- `flow/review/loop.py` (now 948 lines, budget 950): right after `reviewer_objected` is computed, `blockers` is extended
  with `finding_outcome_blockers(reviewers, merge_findings(iter_findings, carried_rows), outcome_dispositions)` where
  `outcome_dispositions = (*all_dispositions, *pending_dispositions, *rejected_history.values())`. One `# why:` line
  records the placement. The blockers then reach `decide_returned_consensus` and `IterationGates`.
- Commit stamping: added `with_commit(dispositions, head_sha)` to `review_finding_outcomes.py` (pure); the loop stamps
  `to_record` with `resolver_outcome.head_sha` when the resolver pushed, so applied rows in `all_dispositions` carry
  `commit_sha`. Unpushed applied fixes stay unstamped and block approval. The ACT-RES ledger row written by
  `DispositionRecorder` has no `commit_sha` field, so no ledger row shape changed (learning target: 0 ledger assertions
  broke; the fallback in item 6 was not needed).
- Learning target result: one test outside the plan's measured list depended on approving after an applied-but-unpushed
  fix: `tests/test_review_carried_findings.py::test_resume_with_an_unpushed_applied_fix_records_it_and_approves`. As the
  plan accepts, it now asserts the opposite and is renamed `..._records_it_and_blocks_approval` (ledger rows and the
  emptied carry-over list are still asserted unchanged). The `resolve_carried_first` docstring sentence that said such a
  fix "does not block approval" was corrected. Both files are outside the plan's touch set; disclosed here.
- `tests/flow/review/test_loop.py`: new `TestEveryFindingNeedsAResolverOutcome` (iteration 1: one nit plus two clean
  reviewers; iteration 2: three clean). A resolver that leaves the nit `open` or applies it without a push ends not
  approved with a blocker naming `R1-F1`; applied with a push or rejected with the reason `inaccurate` approves. The
  five listed tests (ledger-commit class and `TestScopedDeltaReviewInTheLoop`) now run three-reviewer ensembles via a
  new `tests/review_fakes.with_clean_peers` helper.
- Observed limit (not changed, loop budget is 2 lines from frozen): when the gate blocks a clean re-review, the loop
  halts through the "no resolver pass applicable" branch; it does not re-send the undisposed finding to the resolver in
  the same run.
