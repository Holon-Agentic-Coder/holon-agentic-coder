### Strategy Rationale

The intent is small, well-scoped and follows a repeated, proven pattern (`*-self-doc` intents such as
`I-1795543016-ledger-self-doc`, `I-1795547220-knowledge-self-doc`), so it is accepted as is and run as a single plan
with no sub-intents. Test-first ordering makes the acceptance criterion the gate. Steps 3–5 are independent of each
other (they touch disjoint files) and each depends only on the test from Step 2.

Aggregation: `p_success` is the product of the dependent-chain probabilities, softened because failures in Steps 3–5 are
locally repairable: `0.99 * 0.95 * 0.95 * 0.94 * 0.97 * 0.93 = 0.76` as a strict lower bound; with one in-step repair
allowed per step, the judged overall is `0.92`. Entropy is the sum of step entropies:
`0.2 + 0.9 + 1.2 + 1.2 + 0.6 + 0.5 = 4.6`. Cost is the sum: `0.3 + 1.5 + 1.2 + 1.2 + 0.6 + 1.2 = 6.0`. Impact is the sum
of step impacts, `3 + 10 + 9 + 8 + 5 + 6 = 41`, discounted to `35` because the codebase-level value is one slice of
many. Learning value is low (`1.5`): the only new information is whether the lint gate accepts converted `# why:`
reasons. Bottleneck: Step 6 (full suite + lint). Strong assumption: no test asserts on docstring content or `__doc__`
(planning grep found none in `src/` or `tests/`).
