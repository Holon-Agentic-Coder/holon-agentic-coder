What the gate does now:

- A job with a JUnit report is judged on test time: the report's suite time, or the sum of its test case times when it
  has none. The ceiling applies to the same measure. A job without a report is judged on wall clock exactly as before.
- When a baseline record lacks the run's kind of test time, the relative check compares wall clock for that run and the
  verdict line says so.
- A breached limit is judged again with the per-test times. A slowdown spread across the suite passes with a
  `::warning::Runner variance: ...` line that prints the evidence. A slowdown concentrated in a few tests fails and
  names them with their times before and after. Without enough per-test data the breach stands.
- The `main` record carries the optional keys `test_seconds` and `test_seconds_source`; the seven original keys are
  unchanged.

Acceptance criteria, each covered by tests in the two timing test files:

  test-time figures and apply each run's slowdown factor to a synthetic per-test suite; they do not replay the runs' own

- (b) A concentrated slowdown fails and names exactly its tests.
- (c) A summed test time above the ceiling from a concentrated slowdown fails whatever the label; wall clock above the
  ceiling with test time below it passes.
- (d) Old-shape baseline records are read, the old comparison is used and the line says so.
