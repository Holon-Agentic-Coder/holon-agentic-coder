### Strategy Rationale

The previous plan failed only at the touch-set gate. Its execution notes record success on every functional criterion.
Reframing or decomposing would throw that evidence away, so this plan keeps the same five-step design and widens the
touch set to `flow/__init__.py`, which `tests/flow/test_package_exports.py` requires. It also pre-authorises
`flow/stages/execute_state.py` as a split target in case `execute.py` drifts past 431 lines. Step 5 adds an explicit
changed-files self-check.

Aggregation:

- **Entropy:** the sum of the step values, `1.5 + 1.5 + 3.0 + 1.0 + 1.0 = 8.0`, which is within the 15.0 budget.
- **Cost:** the sum, `2 + 2 + 4 + 1 + 1 = 10.0`.
- **Impact:** a qualitative judgement of 75. Making the most-edited stage readable and testable is high value, but no
  user-visible behaviour changes.
- **p_success:** bounded by the bottleneck steps, Step 3 (0.88) and Step 5 (0.92). It is raised to 0.85 overall, rather
  than taken as the product, because the steps are strongly correlated through the replayed, already-validated work.
- **Learning value:** the sum, rounded to 3.0.

`EV = 0.85 * 75.0 + 0.5 * 3.0 - 0.3 * 8.0 - 10.0 = 63.75 + 1.50 - 2.40 - 10.0 = 52.85`.

Strong assumption: the base `main` has not moved enough since `df3b0d10` to change `execute.py`. If it has, the executor
re-applies the design by hand rather than cherry-picking, and Step 3's cost may approach `1.5 * 4.0`.
