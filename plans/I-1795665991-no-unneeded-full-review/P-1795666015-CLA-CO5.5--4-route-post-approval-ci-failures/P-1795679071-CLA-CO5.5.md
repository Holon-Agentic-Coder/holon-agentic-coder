### Strategy Rationale

The intent is the parent plan's Step 4. Its prerequisites have merged on the target branch: the split `DiffGate` and
`PipelineGate` (`flow/review/approval_gates.py`, `PipelineGate.classification` already exists but is never set), the
extracted `flow/review/ci_inspection.py`, and scoped delta review (`flow/review/delta_review*.py`). What remains is the
routing itself.

Two findings from reading the code shape the reframe:

1. `loop.py` is at 936 of its frozen 952 lines and `run_review_loop` already carries a legacy `noqa`. A "skip the
   ensemble on the next pass" branch would need new state across iterations and a new verdict source for an iteration
   without reviewers. Rerunning noise inside the iteration that saw it (after the clean review, before the verdict)
   needs about four lines in `loop.py`, keeps the verdict computed from the real reviewers, and still dispatches zero
   extra ensembles. When the rerun is still red, the existing path already builds `timing_gate_finding` (or
   `ci_failure_finding`) and sends it to the resolver, and the next pass is scoped by `delta_review` because the head
   moved by the fix alone.
2. In Stage 5 the merged head contains the conflict gate's merge commit. `delta_review._decide_from_git` returns `full`
   ("target merge in delta") for any delta with a merge commit and no scope paths. So a defect restart would always get
   a full review unless the delta-review anchor moves to the merged head. That is safe only when the diff gate holds on
   the merged head (same PR-own fingerprint), which is exactly the case the routing handles. A `CHANGED` coverage keeps
   today's withdrawal.

`review_restart.py` is at 197 of 200 lines, so the Stage 5 logic goes into a new `flow/review/merged_head_routing.py`,
and `_stage4_ci_record` moves to `merged_head_gate.py` (which owns the record format) to make room for the call-site
edits.

**Aggregation.** `entropy_pred` is the sum of step entropies: `1.5 + 2.5 + 3.5 + 1.0 + 2.0 = 10.5`. `cost_pred` is the
sum of step costs: `2.5 + 3.0 + 3.5 + 1.0 + 2.0 = 12.0`. `impact_pred` (60) is the step impacts
(`15 + 18 + 20 + 4 + 8 = 65`) discounted for overlap between Steps 2 and 3. `learning_value_pred` is the sum

Step 3 (0.65); the strict product (about 0.31) understates success because each step has a discard path that keeps the
earlier steps shippable, and Step 5 only verifies.

`EV = 0.62 * 60.0 + 0.5 * 3.5 - 0.3 * 10.5 - 12.0 = 37.20 + 1.75 - 3.15 - 12.00 = 23.80`

**Reconciled prior decision.** `ci_timing_finding.py` says a timing-gate failure is fixed in the slowest tests, "never a

**Strong assumptions.**
