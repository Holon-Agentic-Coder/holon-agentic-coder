### Strategy Rationale

The intent is accepted as-is because consolidating per-runner mounts, defaults, harness markers, session detection
paths, and model-discovery metadata onto `RunnerSpec` directly eliminates cross-file duplication across
`agent_defaults.py` and `launcher/mounts.py` at low risk (`entropy_pred = 5.51`, well below the `15.0` budget) and high
utility (`impact_pred = 85.0`).

Two critical architectural constraints shape the design:

1. **Strict acyclicity (`test_import_cycle_ratchet.py`):** Because `launcher/mounts.py` will import `runner_spec_for`
   from `sandbox_executor.runners` (`launcher -> runners`), no module inside `runners/` may import anything from
   `sandbox_executor.launcher` (not even `launcher.antigravity_mounts` or `launcher.pi_mounts`), `agent_defaults`, or
   `agent_runner`. Therefore, `runners/spec.py` defines declarative `SessionMountRule` and `ModelCapability` frozen
   dataclasses, `runners/claude.py` and `runners/codex.py` use declarative `SessionMountRule` tuples, and
   `runners/antigravity.py` and `runners/pi.py` own self-contained session mount builders using only stdlib (`os`,
   `sys`) and `sandbox_executor.local_llm`.
2. **Public symbol and re-export preservation (`test_import_cycle.py`):** `sandbox_executor/cli/__init__.py` re-exports
   `antigravity_session_mounts` and `get_pi_mounts` from `sandbox_executor.launcher.mounts`, and
   `test_model_alias_families.py` / `test_models_cli.py` import `ModelCapability`, `RUNNER_CAPABILITIES`,
   `HARNESS_MARKERS`, `NON_MARKER_VARS`, and `detect_runner_auth_status` from `agent_defaults.py`. `launcher/mounts.py`
   keeps the two re-exports from `launcher/antigravity_mounts.py` and `launcher/pi_mounts.py`, and `agent_defaults.py`
   re-exports `ModelCapability` from `runners.spec` while deriving all tables dynamically from `RUNNER_SPECS`.
