  `patch("shutil.which")` still intercept and unmocked writes still reach the suite write guard.

  tell a spawn failure (timeout, `OSError`, missing binary) from a `gh` non-zero exit. Without it, a timed-out
  `gh pr view` in `open_or_update` would fall through to `gh pr create`, and review/comment exceptions would lose their
  "encountered exception" outcome text; with it both behaviours are preserved.
- Exported `GitHubAdapter`, `parse_gh_json`, `GhResult`, `gh_env` and `spawn_gh` from `flow/__init__.py` (the package
  export ratchet requires it; `KNOWN_UNEXPORTED` unchanged).
