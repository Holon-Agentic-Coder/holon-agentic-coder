## Summary

Adds scoped delta review to Stage 4 (`flow/review/delta_review.py` and its `delta_review_*.py` siblings). After a pass
in which every reviewer completed, the next pass on a descendant head reviews only `last_reviewed_head..head` over the
reviewable pathspecs. It also gets the redacted, bounded full post-change content of the modified files and the findings
the resolver addressed. Every unknown or unsafe state falls back to a full review: limit `0`, an unknown head, rewritten
history, a target merge, an empty or oversize delta, the chain limit of 3, a critical finding on the last pass, or a git
failure. `HOLON_SCOPED_REVIEW_MAX_LINES=0` disables scoped review. The host decides and forwards the decision to the
review-loop container with `--delta-request`.

This re-plan reuses the design the previous attempt (E-1795674437-CLA-CO5.5) implemented and tested on the same base
commit. Its only defect was touch-set admission, and every file is now in this plan's touch set. Changes against that
attempt: the CRITICAL row of the decision table (missing before), the `1000`-byte minimum for
`HOLON_SCOPED_REVIEW_CONTEXT_BYTES`, and tests and docs for both. The changed paths, excluding `plans/`, `executions/`
and `holon-knowledge/`, are exactly 21 files, all in the touch set. `module_budgets.json` and `uv.lock` are unchanged,
and no `# noqa` was added.
